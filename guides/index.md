# A Rough Idea Workshops

These workshops are about designing and prototyping with AI. You'll start with what a language model actually does, design an agent's behavior, build prototypes by describing them, publish them for others to use, and work with a coding assistant as a collaborator. You'll finish by presenting what you made and what it taught you.

Each workshop works on its own, so start wherever you are. Each one has a start page, a **TLDR** for a quick run-through, and detailed guides for a deeper understanding.

## The Introductory Sequence

| # | Workshop | What you'll do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [How It Actually Works](/how-it-works/) | See what a language model actually does, and where an AI product's design lives. |
| 2 | [AI as a Material](/ai-as-material/) | Learn how models are made, then design an agent's behavior by acting it out. |
| 3 | [AI as a Tool](/ai-as-tool/) | Build working prototypes by describing them, and give the tool your design. |
| 4 | [Sharing AI Prototypes](/sharing-prototypes/) | Publish a prototype from AI Studio to GitHub and Vercel, so anyone can open it. |
| 5 | [Prototyping Toolkit](https://prototypingtoolkit.aroughidea.com/) | Set up a coding assistant, GitHub and Vercel, then prototype with them. |
| 6 | [Presenting Your Work](/presenting/) | Show what you built, how you built it, and what it taught you. |

## Across the Workshops

- **[Practice](/practice/).** Keep a process journal, document your design, and test whether your prototype behaves as you intend.
- **[Glossary](/glossary).** The words the workshops use, in plain language.
- **[Going Further](/going-further).** Optional projects for after the sequence.

## What You'll Need

A laptop, a web browser, and free accounts that each workshop names when you need them. Workshop 5 also uses VS Code and a paid coding assistant plan, unless your school provides one.

## Three Ways to Think About AI

- **AI as a tool.** You use it to get things done.
- **AI as a material.** It becomes an ingredient in something you make for other people.
- **AI as a medium.** It becomes a new kind of knowledge artifact in its own right.

The sequence moves through all three.
