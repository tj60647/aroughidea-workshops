# Creating Your Accounts

*This guide covers step 1 of the [TLDR](/sharing-prototypes/tldr), with more explanation.*

---

## What This Is

The pipeline uses three services. Each one does one job:

| Service | Its job |
|---|---|
| **AI Studio** | Where you describe and build your app. |
| **GitHub** | Where your app's files are stored, with a history of every change. |
| **Vercel** | Where your app runs, at an address anyone can open. |

You already have AI Studio from Workshop 3. This guide sets up the other two. If you already have them, skip to the Checkpoint.

---

## Words You'll See

- **Account:** your sign-in for a service. All three services here have free plans.
- **Repository:** a project's folder of files on GitHub, with its history. People often shorten it to "repo".
- **Deploy:** put a version of your app online.

---

## Step 1: Create Your Accounts

### GitHub

1. Go to [GitHub](https://github.com/).
2. Press **Sign up** at the top right.
3. Follow the steps: an email address, a password and a username. Your username appears in your project's web address, so choose one you're happy to share.
4. Confirm your email address when GitHub asks.

### Vercel

1. Go to [Vercel](https://vercel.com/).
2. Press **Sign Up** at the top right.
3. If Vercel asks what you'll use it for, choose the free plan for personal projects.
4. When Vercel asks how to sign up, choose **Continue with GitHub**, and approve the connection on GitHub.

**Why sign up with GitHub?** Vercel then knows which GitHub account is yours, so it can find your repositories later. You can sign up another way, but you'll need to connect GitHub before you can deploy.

---

## Checkpoint

- You can sign in to [GitHub](https://github.com/) and see your username at the top right.
- You can sign in to [Vercel](https://vercel.com/) and see your dashboard.
- You can open your app in AI Studio.

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

**GitHub didn't send a confirmation email.** Check your spam folder, then ask GitHub to send it again from your account settings.

**AI Studio won't open with my school account.** Some schools block it. Use a personal Google account instead.
