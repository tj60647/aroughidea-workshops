# Later Workshops

These workshops follow the introductory sequence. They build on its vocabulary and practice, and their pages are still being written.

## AI as Design Material: Where the Design Decisions Are

AI systems are full of design decisions that shape what people experience: what gets said, how the system responds, what it remembers and what it ignores. This workshop treats the design surfaces from the introductory sequence as authored work, and practices naming, comparing and critiquing the choices in them.

## Agentic System Design

Some systems split a job between several agents that hand work to one another. This workshop looks at how to design those roles and handoffs, and how to tell when responsibility between them has blurred.
