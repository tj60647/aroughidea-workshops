# Practice: AI as a Tool

Do these before the next workshop. Work alone, or build separately with others and compare your apps.

## What to Do

1. **Make a basic app in AI Studio.** Keep it simple, with no AI features, like the camera obscura explainer. See **Building a Basic App in AI Studio**.
2. **Ask AI Studio for a letter-writing assistant with no guidance.** Don't use your role card. Note what AI Studio does on its own. See **Giving AI Studio Your Design**.
3. **Use your role card to build a letter-writing agent.**
   1. Write your design intent.
   2. Describe at least one user journey.
   3. Give AI Studio your role card as the agent's system instructions.
   4. Test the agent with your test script.
   5. Ask for a system diagram behind an info button.
   6. Change the app through conversation with AI Studio, and test again.

   Do the testing and changing at least three times. See **Testing and Revising Your Agent**.
4. **Try a different design goal.** Pick something other than a letter-writing assistant, and follow the same steps: design intent, user journey, role card, test script, system diagram, and three rounds of revision.

Keep a record of each version: its role card, its test results and what you changed. **Documenting an AI Design** and **Testing Your AI Prototype** explain how.

## Journal Prompts

Add a dated entry to your [process journal](/practice/journal). Include a screenshot of each app and its system diagram. Then answer:

- What did AI Studio do differently with your role card than without it?
- Which part of your design was hardest to put into words?
- What did testing show you that building didn't?
- For your second design goal, what carried over from the letter-writing assistant, and what didn't?
- Where did your intent change as you worked? What changed it?
