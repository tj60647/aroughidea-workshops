# Practice: Presenting Your Work

This is the last workshop in the introductory sequence, so there's no next session to prepare for. Use this practice to turn what you made into something you can keep and show.

## What to Do Next

- **Revise your deck with the feedback you got.** Fix the slide people found unclear, and cut anything you rushed through.
- **Turn it into portfolio pages.** Keep the same parts:
  - concept and context: why you made it, your goals, what you made and your inspiration;
  - process and iteration;
  - final prototype, outcome and reflections;
  - photos and screenshots;
  - optionally, links to your code or model files and your demo video.
- **Record a demo video** if you haven't yet. See [Making a Demo Video](/presenting/demo-video).
- **Check your links.** Open your live prototype and your video in a private browser window. Links that work today can stop working when an API key runs out or a free plan changes, so keep your screenshots and video as a record.
- **Pick one next step** from the group discussion and try it. [Going Further](/going-further) has optional projects to start from.

## Journal Prompts

Add a final entry to your [process journal](/practice/journal), tied to Workshop 6:

- What did you present, and what feedback did you get?
- Which themes came up across the presentations, and which ones apply to your project?
- Which frame, tool, material or medium, best describes your prototype now? Has that changed since Workshop 1?
- What would you do differently if you started this project again?
- What will you make next?
