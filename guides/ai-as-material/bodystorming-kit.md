# Bodystorming Kit

*Printable materials for step 3 of the [TLDR](/ai-as-material/tldr). How to use them: [Bodystorming an Agent](/ai-as-material/bodystorming).*

**To print:** use your browser's **Print** command (`Cmd + P` on a Mac, `Ctrl + P` on Windows). Cut each goal card into three pieces: the goal and notes, the reveal note, and the new constraint. The user keeps the last two hidden until their moment.

**Each group needs:** one goal card, one observer sheet for each observer, and one blank role card.

---

## Goal Card A: A Resignation Letter

**Goal:** You're leaving your job. You need to write a resignation letter to your manager.

**Your rough notes:**

- last day… end of next month?
- manager is Dana
- new job, but can't say where yet
- still annoyed about the schedule changes

**Reveal at minute 5:** "I'd like Dana to give me a reference later."

**New constraint at minute 8:** "HR will read this too. It has to be short and formal."

---

## Goal Card B: An Apology to a Colleague

**Goal:** You made a mistake that affected a colleague's work. You want to apologize in writing.

**Your rough notes:**

- sent the client the old file
- Sam redid the slides over the weekend
- does my manager know?
- don't want it to sound like excuses

**Reveal at minute 5:** "Sam already told two people on the team about it."

**New constraint at minute 8:** "My manager wants to be copied on the letter."

---

## Goal Card C: A Request to a Landlord

**Goal:** Something in your apartment is broken. You want your landlord to fix it.

**Your rough notes:**

- heater broken since… the 3rd? maybe earlier
- called once, no answer
- rent is paid
- can't afford a space heater

**Reveal at minute 5:** "My neighbor downstairs has the same problem."

**New constraint at minute 8:** "The landlord only accepts repair requests by email, with dates and photos."

---

## Observer Sheet

**Your lens (circle one):**

- **Interaction:** what had to happen for progress to be made?
- **System:** what would this role need to work reliably?

**Rules:** one idea per sticky note. Write what you see, not what it means. Don't give advice during the session.

**After the session, pick your two or three most important notes.**
