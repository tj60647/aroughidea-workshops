# Practice Across the Workshops

Each workshop ends with a Practice page: what to try before the next one. The pages here are the practice that runs through the whole series. They help you keep a record of your work, describe your design so others can discuss it, and check whether your prototype does what you meant it to.

You'll use all three when you present your work in [Workshop 6](/presenting/).

## The Journal Habit

Keep a process journal from the first workshop to the last. Write at least one dated entry for each workshop, with screenshots, sketches and notes on what surprised you and what broke. A journal is easier to keep as you go than to rebuild at the end, and it becomes the raw material for your case study and your portfolio.
