## Before the Next Workshop

### Iterate on your experiment, and journal it

Go back to your agent in [Agent Studio](https://agentstudio.aroughidea.com/). Change one design surface at a time, run your question buttons again, and record what happened. In your journal, include:

- **a comparison of conversations:** the same questions, answered by two or more versions of your agent, side by side;
- **the addresses of your experiments,** so you and others can open them again. Add a QR code for each, if you like.

### Try other agent sandboxes

Many AI tools have a place where you can write system instructions, change settings and add knowledge. Find one or two, and build the same stand-in for yourself there. Notice which design surfaces each one shows you, and which it hides.

### Watch the 3Blue1Brown video

Watch [3Blue1Brown's video on large language models](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wjZofJX0v4M). It explains the next-token loop visually. It's best watched with others on a big screen, and paused to talk about it.

## Journal Prompts

Write a dated entry in your [process journal](/practice/journal). Use these prompts to start:

- Look back at your answers to "Where are you now?" on the [start page](/how-it-works/). What would you answer differently now?
- Describe the next-token loop in your own words, as if to a friend.
- Which design surface surprised you most, and why?
- Paste in two answers to the same question from different versions of your agent. What explains the difference?
- What would you want to know before trusting an AI product you use every day?
